When I first opened my Ziwei Doushu chart, I had no idea what I was looking at. It looked like a 4×3 grid of Chinese characters with little symbols floating around. There was a circle in the middle. There were numbers in some corners. I closed the tab, convinced this wasn't for me.

This is that walkthrough. If you've just generated your Ziwei chart (or you're about to), here's how to read it.

## Step 1: Understand the Shape

A Ziwei chart is a **4×3 grid** of twelve **palaces** (宫位 / gōng wèi). Four rows, three columns. Total: twelve sectors, each covering a different life theme.

Unlike Western astrology's circular wheel, where houses shift based on your birth time and location, Ziwei's twelve palaces are **fixed relative to the chart's orientation**. The Life Palace doesn't move — but its position in the grid changes based on your birth month and hour.

This means: **the chart itself rotates around the Life Palace**, not around the planets.

The visual is deliberately imperial: palaces are arranged like the imperial court, with the Emperor in the central position, surrounded by ministers, generals, and advisors.

## Step 2: Find the Life Palace (命宫)

The **Life Palace (命宫 / mìng gōng)** is the most important palace in your chart. It represents **you** — your core personality, your default mode of operating in the world.

How to find it on your chart:

1. Look for the Chinese character 命 or the English label "Life".
2. It contains your **Ming Zhu (命主)** — your main star, the dominant star of your personality.
3. The position of the Life Palace in the grid tells you the "flavor" of your chart — the season and time-of-day energy you're most aligned with.

If the Life Palace is at the bottom-center of the chart, your chart reads "more inward, more reflective." If it's at the top-center, your chart reads "more outward, more visible."

The Life Palace star (Ziwei, Tianfu, Pojun, Tianji, etc.) is the **first key insight** into your personality. Each of the 14 main stars has a distinct character.

## Step 3: Find the Body Palace (身宫)

Right next to the Life Palace is the **Body Palace (身宫 / shēn gōng)** — often diagonally adjacent.

The Body Palace represents **how you actually live your life**, as opposed to the Life Palace which represents **how you are at your core**.

The relationship between these two is one of the most useful interpretive tools in Ziwei:

- **Life Palace and Body Palace in the same square** — your core and your lived life are aligned. What you are, you live.
- **Life Palace and Body Palace opposite each other** — there's tension between who you are and how you live. You may spend your life reconciling this.
- **Life Palace and Body Palace adjacent** — close harmony, but some drift between ideal and actual.

For me, Life and Body are diagonally adjacent — meaning my core personality and my actual life path are aligned but not perfectly overlapping. That's been accurate.

## Step 4: Identify Your Main Star (主星)

Each Life Palace contains one or two of the **14 main stars**. These are the dominant forces in your personality.

Here are the 14, in rough order from "most imperial" to "most disruptive":

**Imperial / Regal:**
- **Ziwei (紫微)** — The Emperor. Regal, dignified, expects respect. High standards. Can be lonely at the top.
- **Tianfu (天府)** — The Empress. Stable, generous, tasteful. Good with money and relationships.

**Ministerial / Strategic:**
- **Tianji (天机)** — The Advisor. Strategic, intellectual, analytical. Restless; always thinking.
- **Taiyang (太阳)** — The Sun Star. Bright, generous, public-facing. Burns brightly for others.
- **Wuqu (武曲)** — The General of Wealth. Decisive, sharp with money. Cut-and-dry.
- **Tiantong (天同)** — The Gentle Minister. Easygoing, nurturing, avoids conflict. May lack drive.

**Capable / Independent:**
- **Lianren (廉贞)** — The Officer Star. Passionate, complicated, intense. Strong emotional life.
- **Tianxiang (天相)** — The Deputy. Supportive, refined, diplomatic. Loyal.

**Fighter / Disruptive:**
- **Qi Sha (七杀)** — The General. Brave, solitary, breaks through. Often loners.
- **Pojun (破军)** — The Pioneer. Disruptive, transformative, never rests. Always starting over.
- **Jumen (巨门)** — The Gatekeeper. Talkative, analytical, can be argumentative.

**Wandering / Spiritual:**
- **Tanlang (贪狼)** — The Wanderer. Charismatic, desirous, talented. Can be scattered.
- **Tiancai (天才)** — The Talent Star. Creative, philosophical, slightly otherworldly.
- **Jixing (七杀)** — The Lone Star. (Some lists combine with Qi Sha.)

You don't need to memorize all 14 — but knowing your main star is the start of reading your chart.

## Step 5: Look at the Empty Palaces

Here's something that confuses Western astrologers most: **not every palace has a main star**. Many palaces are "empty."

Don't panic. An empty palace is **not** a weak palace. It means the palace is led by an **auxiliary star (辅星)** or by the **transformations** currently flying through it.

Auxiliary stars include:
- **Left Assistant (左辅)** and **Right Assistant (右弼)** — supportive influences.
- **Wenchang (文昌)** and **Wenqu (文曲)** — literary/creative talents.
- **Huagai (华盖)** — the artist/star, slightly mystical.
- **Tianma (天马)** — the traveling star, mobility and movement.
- **Xianchi (仙池)** and others — various specialized influences.

Empty palaces often represent areas of life where you have **freedom and flexibility** — themes you can shape rather than themes that shape you.

## Step 6: Find the Four Transformations Flying In

The **Four Transformations (四化)** are the dynamic energies that move through your chart over time:

- **化禄 (Huà Lù)** — Prosperity, ease, gifts.
- **化权 (Huà Quán)** — Power, authority, decisiveness.
- **化科 (Huà Kē)** — Fame, recognition, refinement.
- **化忌 (Huà Jì)** — Friction, lessons, integration.

Each year, decade, or luck pillar activates a different transformation in a different palace. The transformation currently flying into your Life Palace is the **most actionable piece of information** in your chart right now:

- Lu in Life Palace — prosperous period for personal goals.
- Quan in Life Palace — time to take charge, claim authority.
- Ke in Life Palace — time of recognition and visibility.
- Ji in Life Palace — time to integrate a lesson, not push.

Looking at the **decade pillar (大运)** currently active in your chart tells you the transformation energy shaping your next 10 years.

## Step 7: Check Your Career Palace (官禄宫) and Wealth Palace (财帛宫)

Two palaces most people want to know about:

**Career Palace (官禄宫)** — your professional life, your public role, your achievements.
**Wealth Palace (财帛宫)** — your money, your relationship with material resources, your financial patterns.

These two palaces, along with the Life Palace, form a **triangle of personal destiny**. Their stars and transformations tell you how to navigate work and money.

A strong main star in Career Palace suggests a clear professional path. An empty Career Palace doesn't mean no career — it means flexibility. The transformations flying in tell you the timing.

## Step 8: Read the Decade Pillar (大运)

Once you've mapped the static chart, the next layer is **time**. Ziwei Doushu divides your life into roughly 10-year chunks called **decade luck pillars (大运)**.

Each pillar is governed by:
1. A **palace** (your Life Palace moves through different palace-energies over the decades).
2. A set of **transformations** (Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji flying into specific palaces).
3. A **stem-branch** (天干地支) that further colors the energy.

The first pillar is roughly your childhood origin. The second is your emergence into adult life. The third is your prime career-building decade. The fourth is your midlife consolidation. Etc.

Most major life events fall at the **boundaries between pillars** — when the palace-energy shifts and the transformations rearrange.

## Putting It All Together

Reading a Ziwei chart isn't about memorizing rules. It's about **building a mental model** of the system:

1. **The 12 palaces** are fixed life themes.
2. **The 14 main stars** are personality archetypes that move through the palaces.
3. **The auxiliary stars** add texture and specialization.
4. **The Four Transformations** are dynamic energies that change with time.
5. **The decade pillars** add long-term timing.

Once you have this skeleton, each chart becomes a specific instance — and you can read it.
